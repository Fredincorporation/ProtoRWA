SECTION 6.0 Physical Risk Metrics

Risk Disclosures on Physical Hardware Manufacturing

Hardware investments carry intrinsic physical vulnerabilities not present in purely software protocols. These include:

  • Global semiconductor supply chain disruptions and wafer fabrication halts.
  • Maritime customs impoundments, tariffs, and geopolitical export controls.
  • Tooling recalibration delays extending lead times by up to 180 calendar days.
  • Loss of physical collateral in transit prior to arrival at bonded warehouse facilities.
