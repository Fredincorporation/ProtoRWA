ProtoRWA Product-Level Real World Assets for Physical Products One-Sentence Description ProtoRWA is a platform that allows founders to raise capital against a specific physical product by issuing tokenized revenue-share claims, with transparent on-chain milestone escrow, investor governance, and secondary market liquidity. The Problem Raising capital for physical products is broken. Traditional options force founders into equity dilution, high-interest debt, or crowdfunding platforms that offer little ongoing transparency or structured investor protections. Investors, meanwhile, struggle to access early-stage physical product opportunities with clear economic rights, verifiable progress, and any path to liquidity. Most existing RWA platforms focus on real estate, invoices, or financial instruments. Almost none address the massive market of individual physical products — the solar refrigerator, the new hardware device, the specialized consumer good — in a transparent, programmable way. The Solution ProtoRWA turns a specific physical product into a fundable, tradable, on-chain asset. Founders create a structured product project, define clear milestones, and issue claim tokens that represent a share of that product’s future revenue or profit. Investor capital is locked in a milestone-based escrow. Funds are only released when investors vote that the agreed milestones have been met. Once claims are live, they can be traded on an internal secondary market, giving early backers a path to liquidity without waiting for a full company exit. How It Works For Founders Create a project and upload a pitch video, product description, images, and key specifications. Define funding goal, revenue-share terms, and milestone schedule (e.g., Prototype → Production Setup → First Production Run → Commercial Launch). AI assistance helps structure the project, suggest realistic milestones, generate clear descriptions, and outline basic economics. Once live, receive capital into escrow as investors buy claim tokens. Submit evidence for each milestone and receive funds only after investor approval. Post ongoing updates on a dedicated project page. Optionally list the finished product for sale later (roadmap), with revenue flowing back to claim holders. For Investors Browse structured product projects with clear terms, pitch videos, and milestone plans. Commit USDC and receive claim tokens representing economic rights to that specific product’s revenue or profit. Vote on milestone completions using voting power proportional to claims held. Track progress through transparent on-chain records and founder updates. Trade claim tokens on the internal secondary market for liquidity. Receive distributions according to the predefined terms as the product generates revenue. Core Features (MVP) Structured project pages with pitch video, media, milestones, and live updates Tokenized product-level revenue/profit-share claims Milestone-based USDC escrow with investor-weighted voting Evidence submission and transparent on-chain governance Basic internal secondary marketplace for claim tokens AI-assisted project creation and structuring Founder and investor dashboards Deployed on Robinhood Chain (with Arbitrum compatibility) Why This Is Unique Product-level, not company-level: Investors fund and own economic rights to a specific product rather than the entire company. Physical-product focus: Expands RWAs beyond traditional financial and real-estate assets into the real economy of manufactured goods. Investor-governed capital release: Milestone verification is controlled by the people who provided the capital. Built-in secondary liquidity: Claim tokens can be traded, solving one of the biggest problems with early-stage physical product investing. Transparent full lifecycle: From idea and funding through production milestones to potential revenue distribution — all visible on-chain. Technical Foundation Smart contracts for claim tokens, milestone escrow, voting, and secondary listings USDC as the primary funding currency IPFS for evidence and media storage Primary deployment on Robinhood Chain for strong RWA and sponsor alignment Clean, modular architecture designed for future expansion (multi-chain, advanced AI agents, e-commerce layer, optional privacy features) Long-Term Vision ProtoRWA aims to become the default infrastructure for product-level capital formation and liquidity. Over time the platform will expand to include deeper AI-assisted product development tools, supplier coordination, a full commerce layer where finished products can be sold with automatic revenue routing to claim holders, advanced verification options, and institutional-grade features. The goal is a complete, transparent pathway from physical product idea to funded, manufactured, and commercially successful product — with aligned incentives between founders and investors at every step. Why Arbitrum & Robinhood Chain Arbitrum provides the scalable, secure, and institutionally trusted environment required for real economic activity. Robinhood Chain, purpose-built for tokenized real-world assets, offers native alignment with the RWA thesis and strong ecosystem support. Together they give ProtoRWA the technical foundation and strategic positioning needed to move from hackathon prototype to real infrastructure.
