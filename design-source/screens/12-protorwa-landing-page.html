How ProtoRWA Operates in 4 Steps

A closed-loop protocol engineering trust between hardware engineers, global audit networks, and decentralized capital.

01 receipt_long

BOM Tokenization

Founders ingest complete Bill of Materials (BOM), tooling CAD files, and component schedules into our Underwriting Studio. The smart contract generates tranche thresholds.

Output: Tranche-Gated ERC-4626 Vault
02 lock

Milestone-Locked Escrow

Backers deposit USDC into Robinhood Subnet multisig escrow contracts. Capital is strictly segmented: 30% Tooling, 40% Raw Assembly, 30% Final Logistics & QA.

Factory inspectors (SGS, TÜV SÜD), 4K CNC logs, and warehouse IoT sensory payloads are cryptographically hashed and submitted onto IPFS via Chainlink Oracles.

Attestation: ISO 9001 Factory Feeds

Automated Yield & Trade
