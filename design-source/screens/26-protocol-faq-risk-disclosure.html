Understanding Product-Level Claims

2 Articles

A ProtoRWA claim token confers direct programmatic cash-flow rights to the gross revenue generated by specific hardware production batches (e.g., Industrial IoT Transceivers Batch 004), rather than shares in the corporate entity.

Product Claim Model Direct priority disbursement capped at target return (typically 1.8x – 2.5x principal). Zero equity dilution, immediate cash velocity.
Traditional Equity Dilutive cap-table rights, dependent on multi-year liquidation events (M&A/IPO), board politics, and residual liquidation preference.

Claim holders have strictly defined voting powers over hardware milestone disbursements and supplier attestations, without operational overhead or general liability for corporate activities.

Revenues generated from authenticated wholesale purchase orders and Shopify/ERP integrations are deposited into institutional treasury clearing vaults. The smart contract router automatically converts fiat or native stablecoins into USDC.

User_Payout = (GrossBatchRevenue * RevenueSharePct) * (ClaimTokensHeld / TotalClaimSupply)

Funds land directly in connected wallets on the Robinhood Subnet with zero gas fees incurred by recipient underwriters. Claim distributions happen bi-weekly or instantaneously upon certified wholesale clearinghouse receipt.

Milestone-Gated Escrow & Governance

3 Articles

Capital is never issued in lump sums. It is segregated into multi-tier tranches controlled by the ProtoRWA Smart Escrow Engine. If an inspection fails (e.g., surface mount assembly failure rate exceeds tolerance bounds) or token holders reject the release package:

  1. Remediation Grace (30 Calendar Days): The manufacturer has 30 days to rectify physical defects and submit newly verified metrology telemetry.
  2. Secondary Audit Trigger: An independent ISO-accredited inspection is commissioned automatically at founder expense.
  3. Automated Clawback Execution: If the re-vote fails to clear the quorum, the remaining unspent tranche balances in escrow execute a self-settling clawback, reimbursing token holders pro-rata in USDC.

Governance enforces mathematical equity: 1 Claim Token = 1 Vote. Milestone approval requires a strict threshold to prevent founder coercion and speculative obstruction:

Required Affirmative Quorum 60.00%
Snapshot Time-Lock: 48 Hours Flash-loan resistant block height

Token snapshots are recorded at the exact moment the verification proof is indexed on-chain. Claim tokens bought after the proof publication cannot participate in that specific tranche's governance vote.

To prevent fraudulent attestations, milestone submissions must bundle three cryptographically pinned data structures to IPFS / Arweave before triggering a validator vote:

verified SGS / TÜV Reports Certified quality assurance testing & metallurgical logs
videocam 4K Machine Telemetry Timestamped CNC, SMT pick-and-place optic feeds
sensors Chainlink IoT Feeds Warehouse RFID dock scans & bill of lading signatures

precision_manufacturing Physical Manufacturing & Yield Degradation Risks

Unlike purely digital synthetic assets, product-level claims depend upon tangible engineering workflows. Unforeseen microchip fabricator halts, foundry defects, raw material tariff fluctuations, and tooling failures can delay batch completion schedules. While multi-sig milestone escrow shields unreleased tranches, historical yield projections do not guarantee future retail sell-through velocities.

hub Oracle & Cross-Jurisdictional Enforcement Limits

ProtoRWA bridges automated smart escrow to off-chain logistics via factory sensor inputs and accredited inspection signatures (SGS, TÜV). In the catastrophic event of manufacturer bankruptcy, claims are governed by Delaware Series LLC statutory liquidation terms. Physical asset repossession or secondary inventory auction involves jurisdiction-specific legal delays.

badge Accreditation & Jurisdictional Restrictions
